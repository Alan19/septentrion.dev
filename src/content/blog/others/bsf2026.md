I went to my second regionals last weekend! It was quite fun, but it felt much harder than last year. It feels like most matchups was me trying to outpace my opponent's resource generation, but I was at a disadvantage this time, especially against decks like Liael=Odium. Odium can generate quite a lot of resources and then finish you off with her Divine Skill on turn 4, which means you don't get any time to whittle down their hand after.

- R1: Odium ❌
- R2: Youthberk F ✅
- R3: Alden ✅
- R4: Odium ❌
- R5: Opponent didn't show up ✅
- R6: Dekka-kun ❌
- R7: Heartluru ❌

Day 2 Matchups:
