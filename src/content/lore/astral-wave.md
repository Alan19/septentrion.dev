# Society in the 27th Century
Astral Wave takes place in the 27th century, where the world has become an almost utopian solarpunk society that guarantees everyone's basic needs. On average, people world 12-20 hours a week, and spend the rest of their time on personal endeavors. Many spend their time relaxing and hanging out with friends, participating in social activities, or volunteering to make their communities better. However, just as many end up spending their free time to researching technologies and innovations that would benefit society, or honing their skills to their absolute limits. This leads to a large network of people skilled in overlapping areas who often collaborate with each other on interdisciplinary projects.

The rapid innovation in society and the large and diverse networks of skilled professionals are made possible with the society's education system. The educational system starts by allowing kids to try out different professions and see what kind of skillsets they want to build. Kids are also giving kid-friendly tools and a wide latitude for self-expression, to build introductory experience in many areas. At 16, they are allowed to choose a goal or profession and enroll in classes and apprenticeships to begin the process of being certified to work on it at a professional level. Mandatory education ends at 18, but most continue either formal education until 24 or rotate through a series of apprenticeships if their desired area of expertise is niche enough to not have an education program around it. The apprenticeships continue until they have the skillset necessary to make independent progress and a support network of other professionals who can assist them. Many change their profession once or twice before settling on one, but benefit from previous apprenticeships / courses as the knowledge and network gained from those can transfer to related fields.

# Astral Plane
The abundance of resources due to the discovery of the Astral Plane, a realm of humanity's collective unconscious, in the early 22nd century due to an incident with quantum computing. This resulted in a tear in reality that caused monsters to invade many major cities, rendering them uninhabitable. However, the invasion allowed humanity to discover exotic materials that produced rapid breakthroughs in fields such as computing, energy storage. This resulted in major advancements in robotics, biology, and computing, allowing for things such as the automation of labor-intensive tasks, increases in healthspan, cognitive-enhancing equipment, morphological freedom, and the eradication of most serious illness. 

By the 27th century, humanity has slowly colonized the Astral Plane, with the colonized areas being dubbed "Astral Waves". Those areas on the outskirts of city centers allow for society to continue exploiting and harvesting the resources from the Astral Plane safely, but require a standing defense force to make sure that the area does not get invaded. The training process for this has been heavily gamified into "Wave Skirmishes", a wargame / combat sport that trains people to be able to fight and perform technical tasks at the same time. In "Wave Skirmishes" (and in defending the Astral Waves in general), combatants are organized crews of 1-5 people known as "Remediators" who pilot bodies commonly called "Projections". Remediators are directed by an operator directing them to other objectives that include fighting other crews or installing infrastructure. 

Around 5% of the population are Remediators or Operators who fight in the Astral Plane, while another 20% of the population is in industries supporting them, such as making / maintaining equipment, refining Projections, conducting environmental surveys, etc.
